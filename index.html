Meet your coach

My name is Thanaphon, but everyone calls me Boss. I grew up in Bangkok and played football until a knee injury at 24. A good rehab coach is what got me moving comfortably again, and I liked it so much that I trained to do the same for others. I've been coaching ever since, first at a clinic gym in Sukhumvit and, since 2022, privately at my clients' hotels, homes and clubs.

FAQ

Good questions,
honest answers.

Will it always be you who coaches me?

Yes. I'm the only coach. Every session, message and plan comes from me, so you never have to explain yourself twice.

I haven't exercised in years. Is that OK?

Absolutely. Most of my clients are not athletes. We start gently and only move on when you feel ready.

Is it safe if I have pain or an injury?

Careful, well-chosen exercise often helps, but I'm a rehab exercise coach, not a doctor or physiotherapist. I don't diagnose. After a recent injury or surgery I'll ask for your doctor's or physio's OK first and follow their advice. If something needs medical attention, I'll tell you.

How soon will I see results?

Honestly, it depends on you. Most people feel less pain and stiffness within 8–10 sessions. Lasting changes in strength and movement usually take around three months of training twice a week.

Do you bring equipment to hotels?

Yes. I bring a mat, bands, a foam roller and small weights, which is all most rehab work needs. You only need about 2 × 3 metres of clear floor.

Which areas of Bangkok do you cover?

Sukhumvit, Thonglor, Ploenchit, Sathorn, Silom, the Riverside and the rest of central Bangkok, with no travel fee. Further out, just ask.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, close-fitting clothes and bare feet or grip socks. I bring the mat, equipment and a clean towel. Try to eat lightly at least an hour before.

What if I need to cancel?

No problem. Reschedule for free up to 12 hours before. Later than that, I charge 50%, because I've kept that hour for you.
