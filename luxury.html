Concierge

Questions, answered.

Do you bring equipment to my hotel?

Yes. Boss brings a mat, bands, a foam roller and light weights, which is all most rehab work needs. You need only a clear space of about 2 × 3 metres. We coordinate service-lift access with your hotel directly.

Is rehab exercise safe after an injury or surgery?

Every new client completes a health screening at the assessment. After a recent injury or surgery, Boss asks for clearance from your doctor or physio and works within their advice from the very first session. Exercise supports recovery; it doesn't replace medical treatment, and Boss does not diagnose.

Which areas of Bangkok do you cover?

Sukhumvit, Thonglor, Ploenchit, Sathorn, Silom, the Riverside and the rest of central Bangkok, with no travel fee. Hua Hin, Pattaya and Phuket residences, and yachts, can be arranged on request.

I haven't exercised in years. Is this for me?

Absolutely. Most of our clients are not athletes. Private coaching is the safest way to start again, because every exercise is adapted to you from the first minute.

How is my privacy protected?

Boss signs a confidentiality agreement on request. We never photograph, name or post about a client without written permission, and your health information is stored securely and never shared.

What should I wear, and what do I need to prepare?

Comfortable, close-fitting clothing and grip socks, which we can supply. We bring the equipment, towels and water. Please eat lightly at least an hour before.

What is your cancellation policy?

You can reschedule for free up to 12 hours before your session. Later cancellations are charged at 50%, and no-shows in full, because your coach and the equipment have been reserved for you alone.
